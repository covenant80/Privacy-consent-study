▦ STUDENT PLANNER

Privacy and Data Processing Consent

By proceeding, you acknowledge that the application processes your email address for account administration, authentication, and account recovery. Subject to your separate selection below, the application may also collect basic application usage information, including feature interactions, for service evaluation and analytics.

The email address is retained while the account remains active and is deleted within 30 days after account deletion. If optional analytics is enabled, basic usage events are retained for up to 12 months. In this fictional demonstration, no external analytics provider receives the usage events. Data is not used for advertising and precise location is not collected for these purposes.

The user may decline optional analytics without losing access to the planner. Consent preferences may be amended or withdrawn at any time through Settings → Privacy. For questions about this demonstration, contact privacy@example.invalid.

This selection is optional and is not required to create or use an account.
